COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers, no fine print.

Do I need an account?

No. Basic conversions work immediately without registration.

What happens to my files?

They are kept temporarily for processing and removed automatically after download or scheduled cleanup.

Will conversion reduce image quality?

Not by default. JPG and WebP are compressed formats, so their selected quality affects size and fidelity. PNG output remains lossless unless palette compression is selected.

How does OCR work?

OCR reads text from image and PDF pages, then provides it as a downloadable text file. Results are strongest for clear, high-resolution documents in English.
